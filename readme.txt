=== KASS-mtv ===

The KASS-mtv Diaspora News theme for kassmtvusa.com.

== Settings ==
Appearance > Customize > KASS-mtv settings:
headline words, mission, email, phone, hours, YouTube / Facebook /
Instagram / LinkedIn / X links, a showreel video, and the KASS BUILDING
line (its card stays hidden while empty).

== Menus ==
Until a menu is chosen under Appearance > Menus ("KASS-mtv: top menu"),
the header shows: News, Platforms, Services, About, Contact.

== News ==
The homepage fills itself from the posts: newest post = Top story,
then the latest stories. Give every post a featured image and a category.

== 1.2: Newsletter, story tips, newsroom pages, speed ==
* Newsletter sign-up on the homepage and under every story. Sign-ups are
  kept in WP Admin > Subscribers, with a "Download all (CSV)" button for
  Mailchimp, Hostinger Reach or any email tool.
* Story tips: the "Send us a story tip" page holds the form
  ([kassmtv_tip_form]). Tips are kept in WP Admin > Story tips and the
  site email gets an alert. The newsletter form is [kassmtv_newsletter].
* Newsroom pages (About the newsroom, Editorial standards, Corrections,
  Send us a story tip) are made once as drafts. Review, fill the
  [brackets] and publish; published ones appear in the footer.
* Speed: Slider Revolution and Contact Form 7 files load only on pages
  that use them; no emoji script; fewer font weights; the top photo
  loads first.
